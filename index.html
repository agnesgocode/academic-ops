GMB Overview & Definition

Google My Business (Google Business Profile)

DEFINITION

What is GMB?

Google My Business (GMB) — officially known as Google Business Profile — is Google's platform for local business listings across Google Search and Google Maps.

SHORT EXPLANATION

Why GMB Matters for Branches

GMB showcases branch locations, contact channels, parent & student ratings, and customer reviews. Actively replying to reviews boosts local search rank and parent confidence.

STRATEGIC AIM

Goals & Key Objectives

  • Local Search Visibility: Capture local parent searches for English learning centers in Regional Indonesia Timur.
  • Trust & Engagement: Maintain 100% review reply rate (Reply Ulasan) to build parent trust.
  • Milestone Attainment: Drive all branches to reach key review milestones: 50, 75, and 100+ reviews.
DATA UPDATE SCHEDULE

Update Frequency

GMB review count and response tracking data are updated every 2nd and 4th week of the month (W2 & W4) from the central Marketing Tracker spreadsheet.
